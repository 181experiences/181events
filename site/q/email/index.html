Community Dinner

A Night in Mexico City

When
Friday, Sept 25
6:00 PM – 8:00 PM
Where
Level 39, Residents’ Club
Seats
12 · $75 per person
Hosted by
Resident Experiences

The marquee evening of our season. A tribute to Michelin-level, Latin-inspired cooking, drawing on the techniques of Californios here in San Francisco and the celebrated kitchens of Mexico City.

Twelve seats at one table on Level 39, with the city forty floors below. Courses are served family-style where the food calls for it, and plated where it doesn’t. Each one is built around a single idea carried from a particular kitchen.

$75 per person. Seats are released on a first-come basis, and we expect this one to fill quickly.

Your seat is held.

Payment of $75 completes the reservation. You’ll be taken to a secure checkout, and a receipt and confirmation will follow by email. Seats are released if payment isn’t completed within 48 hours.

When
Wednesday, Oct 7
6:00 PM – 7:30 PM

Level 39, Residents’ Club

20 places
RSVP by

Hosted by
Resident Experiences

A succulent-topped pumpkin to carry your home through the autumn. You’ll design your own, and learn how to keep the cuttings healthy along the way.

We begin with a demonstration and a proper talk on succulent care, then everyone builds theirs. Mini pumpkins and all materials are provided, so there’s nothing to bring.

When the pumpkin has had its run, often many months from now, the cuttings can be replanted and enjoyed for years. Light bites and wine will be served throughout.
