The doctrine mirrors Events: Publish is the only road onto the site, Unpublish takes a page off the air with its content held, Archive files an unpublished page away, and Delete exists for drafts only, since residents never saw one. Every change is stamped with who and when, and notifies, like everything else.

What the real build is, in one breath: a pages table in the database (title, address, status, blocks, who and when), the same API verbs as events, this screen in the admin, and the publisher renders blocks into the exact house template Fleet Week uses today. Pages ride the same pipeline as everything else: publish rebuilds the site, every change notifies and is stamped, drafts delete, published pages unpublish then archive. Nothing on the resident side changes; the pages simply stop needing a programmer.
