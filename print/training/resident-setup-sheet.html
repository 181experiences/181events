Helping residents set up 181residents.com

Residents have been invited by email to put the events calendar on their phones. Expect two asks at the desk: my code, please and help me put it on my phone. Both take under a minute.

1 · The code card

Each residence has one card with its sign-in code; hand the household theirs. The code is typed once, when they first RSVP or sign in, and the device then stays signed in for a month at a time. Capital or small letters, with or without the dash: it works however it comes out.

No card, or lost? Residents tab, find the name, and Email code sends their standing code to the address on file, from your own mailbox. Print makes a fresh card for a walk-up. For a caller you have not verified, email beats reading it aloud, always.

2 · Putting it on their phone

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Go to 181residents.com
  2. Tap the … button in the bar at the bottom
  3. Tap Share (square with the up arrow)
  4. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add

Older Safari shows the Share button right in the bar; start at step 3.

Android phones (Chrome)

  1. Go to 181residents.com
  2. Tap the three dots in the top corner
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add

Some Androids word it “Install” or “Add shortcut”; any of them is right.

3 · Sounds broken, isn’t

“There’s no Share button.” It hides behind the … button on newer iPhones; step 2 above.
“The icon didn’t appear.” It lands on the last page of their home screen; swipe left.
“It forgot me.” Normal after a month away or on a new device. They enter the same code again; nothing was lost.
“Do I need the app store?” No. There is nothing to download; the icon simply opens the site.
