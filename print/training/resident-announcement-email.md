Dear neighbors,

I'm delighted to share something we've been building for you: **181residents.com**, The Residents' Club calendar, in your pocket. Every gathering, dinner, and class at the Club lives there, with RSVP built in. Tap any event, tap RSVP, and you're on the list; you can bring your party, see everything you've signed up for under My RSVPs, and add any event straight to your own calendar.

**No code is needed to look around.** The calendar is open to everyone at the building; browse it today. A personal code comes into play only the first time you RSVP to an event.

**When you're ready to RSVP, your code is waiting.** Each residence has its own card at the front desk; pick yours up whenever you're next passing through the lobby. There's no hurry and no line to join: if you'd rather not stop by, email the desk at **concierge@181sf.com** or text the concierge phone, and we'll send your code to you. You'll enter it just once; after that, the site remembers you on your device for a month at a time.

**To keep it on your home screen like an app:**

*On iPhone or iPad:*
1. Open Safari and go to **181residents.com**
2. Tap the **⋯** button in the bar at the bottom of the screen
3. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow pointing up)
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen** (the square with the + sign), then **Add**

If your Safari shows the Share button right in the bar, skip the ⋯ and start there.

*On Android phones:*
1. Open Chrome and go to **181residents.com**
2. Tap the three dots in the top corner
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**, then **Add**

From then on it opens with one tap, just like any app.

If anything gives you trouble, the front desk can help, or write to me directly through the Message page on the site. I read every note.
